Book on carxq.com in a few minutes: set pickup and return dates, choose a car with live season pricing, pick LGK Door 3, Kuah Jetty, or a hotel-list property, add extras if needed, then pay online or choose pay-at-pickup where offered.

## Step-by-step

1. Enter dates and times on the homepage booking dock
2. Browse fleet cards with Low / Peak / Super Peak rates for those dates
3. Select pickup and return locations
4. Add child seat (RM30) or second driver (RM20) if required
5. Apply a promo code only when one is actually live
6. Pay by card or FPX online, or use pay-at-pickup when the flow offers it

## After you book

You receive a confirmation with a booking reference. Message flight or ferry changes early so Door 3 or jetty timing can move with you. Manage the booking from your customer account after login.

## Modify or cancel

Follow the published [refund policy](/en/refund-policy): cancellations 14+ days before pickup can qualify for a full rental refund (less the RM30 administrative fee); 7–13 days are partial; inside 7 days are generally non-refundable; no-shows are forfeited. Promotional bookings marked non-refundable stay non-refundable. Early vehicle return does not automatically refund unused days.
