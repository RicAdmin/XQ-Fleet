You do not need a different rental product for an early arrival at Langkawi International Airport (LGK). Select airport pickup and a realistic meeting time, confirm the handover in advance, share your flight number, then collect your bags and walk left through Arrivals to **Door 3**.

## Confirm the Door 3 handover before you fly

XQ Car's current site describes Door 3 service as available 24/7, but an early pickup should not be treated as a walk-in counter. Check that your confirmation shows the correct date, time and **Langkawi Intl Airport · Door 3**. Very early and late-night handovers need to be arranged before travel.

Check the confirmed price as carefully as the meeting time. XQ Car's marketing pages describe the airport meet as free, while the current [rental agreement](/en/rental-agreement) says after-hours pickup or return must be pre-arranged and may carry a service fee. The written booking total is the practical answer for your rental.

## Choose an exit time, not just the scheduled landing time

A domestic passenger normally reaches Arrivals faster than an international passenger who must clear immigration, collect luggage and pass customs. When choosing the pickup time, allow for the part of the journey between wheels-down and walking out of the terminal.

The sequence is straightforward:

1. Share your flight number and expected arrival time with the pickup team.
2. After landing, complete immigration, baggage claim and customs where applicable.
3. Enter the Arrivals hall and walk left to Door 3.
4. Meet the team and inspect the car before accepting the keys.

If your flight is delayed, send the revised arrival time rather than relying only on the original schedule. This gives the team a usable handover time. For photographs and the full meeting-point explanation, see the [LGK Door 3 pickup guide](/en/blog/langkawi-airport-car-rental-pickup).

## Prepare these items before baggage claim ends

- Booking confirmation and pickup contact
- Original driving licence
- Passport or MyKad
- Credit card for the security deposit or pre-authorisation
- Flight number and revised arrival time, if delayed
- An offline map and your hotel's saved pin

Do not rely on softcopies alone. Check the licence, age, International Driving Permit and deposit rules in the [Langkawi rental requirements guide](/en/blog/rent-a-car-langkawi-requirements). If your paperwork is unusual, resolve it before the flight rather than at Door 3.

## Set up your phone and first route inside the terminal

Mobile-data counters may not match the timing of an early arrival, and roaming can take a while to connect. Open your offline map before leaving the terminal and save the exact hotel pin, not only the hotel name.

From the airport roundabout, follow signs west for Pantai Cenang or east for Kuah. Traffic keeps **left** in Malaysia. Quiet roads at dawn can make it easy to relax too early, so take particular care with lane choice at the first roundabout. The [first-time Langkawi driving guide](/en/blog/driving-langkawi-first-time) covers the rest of that first journey.

## Plan an early return backwards from airline check-in

Pre-arrange the return as well as the pickup. XQ Car's current airport guidance recommends reaching Door 3 about **30 minutes before you intend to begin airline check-in** so there is time for the vehicle inspection and deposit paperwork. That is not your airport-security or airline buffer; allow separate time for those queues.

Before handing over the car, check the fuel level, existing marks and every luggage compartment. Do not rush into the terminal until both sides have completed the inspection. Confirm any after-hours return arrangement or fee in the booking before the final morning.

Once the Door 3 location and time are settled, complete the [XQ Car booking](/en#booking-dock). The useful early-arrival checklist is short: a confirmed handover, original documents, a live flight update and a route that works without mobile data.
