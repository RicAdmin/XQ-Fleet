XQ Car meets Langkawi International Airport (LGK) bookings at Door 3 Arrivals around the clock when airport pickup is selected — free, with your flight number on the booking so delays do not strand the handover.

## Where Door 3 is

Clear immigration and baggage claim, then exit into the Arrivals hall. Door 3 is on the **left** in Arrivals. Look for an XQ Car sign with your name. Domestic belts are a short walk; international arrivals add queue time before you reach the hall.

## Have these ready

- Booking confirmation (email or account)
- Passport or MyKad and your driving licence (physical cards)
- Credit card for the security deposit or pre-authorisation
- Flight number already on the booking if you entered it online

Document rules: [requirements guide](/en/blog/rent-a-car-langkawi-requirements). Cover and excess: [insurance explained](/en/blog/langkawi-car-rental-insurance).

## Walk to the car and drive out

Staff walk you to the vehicle in the short-term parking area by the terminal. First-time visitors: leave the airport roundabout and follow signs west to Pantai Cenang or east to Kuah. Download offline maps if your SIM data is not live yet — see [first drive tips](/en/blog/driving-langkawi-first-time).

## Returning to LGK

Return at the same Door 3 meeting point unless you arranged hotel collection. Allow about **30 minutes before check-in** for inspection and deposit paperwork. Deposit release timing depends on your card issuer — typically several business days after final adjustments (see terms). Early-morning flights are covered; there is no separate after-hours airport surcharge for Door 3 meets on XQ Car bookings.
