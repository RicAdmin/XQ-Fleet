Base in Pantai Cenang and a rental makes short hops to Tengah, Oriental Village, and Kuah practical — as long as you treat evening parking as a scarce resource. Select your hotel from the XQ Car booking list for free delivery; custom-typed addresses may incur the hotel delivery fee (see [hotel delivery](/en/blog/langkawi-hotel-car-delivery)).

## Parking on the strip

After about 17:00, marked hotel bays and commercial lots behind the main road fill fast. Park once and walk. Do not block shop shutters or motorcycle bays. Keep valuables out of sight for beach walks.

## Timing to SkyCab / Oriental Village

Allow roughly 40 minutes off-peak and more in evening congestion. Leave before 09:00 for morning cable-car slots if that is your priority day. Narrow hill approaches are easier in daylight for first-time drivers — [driving tips](/en/blog/driving-langkawi-first-time).

## Car class for a Cenang base

Axia or Bezza is enough for couples. Families should read [best family cars](/en/blog/best-car-langkawi-family-trip). For a three-day loop from Cenang, use the [3-day itinerary](/en/blog/langkawi-3-day-itinerary-by-car).
