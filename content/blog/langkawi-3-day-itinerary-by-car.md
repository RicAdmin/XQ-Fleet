A three-day Langkawi self-drive works best from a Cenang or Kuah base with one west-coast day, one cable-car / waterfall day, and one east or north nature day. Expect about 40–90 minutes of driving on the heavier days, not a full-island marathon every morning.

## Day 1 — West coast settle-in

Morning: beach time or an indoor stop if you landed late. Afternoon: short hop Cenang → Pantai Tengah. Evening: stay on the strip. Driving stays under about 20 km. A compact automatic is enough; families may prefer Alza-class from day one if luggage is heavy.

## Day 2 — Oriental Village, SkyCab, optional waterfall

Leave early for parking and morning cable-car slots at Oriental Village. Add Telaga Tujuh only if legs and weather cooperate. Fill fuel in Cenang or Padang Matsirat before you climb toward viewpoint roads. Return before dark if you are new to left-hand hill driving.

## Day 3 — Kilim boat day or north beach

Pick one: park at Kilim for a mangrove boat tour, or drive the north-east for a quieter beach afternoon. Trying both plus Kuah shopping usually ruins the day. If you fly out next morning, return via a known fuel station and allow Door 3 inspection time — [airport return](/en/blog/langkawi-airport-car-rental-pickup).

## Car choice

Couples: Axia or Bezza. Family of five+: Alza or larger — [family guide](/en/blog/best-car-langkawi-family-trip). Wet forecast: keep the same car; change the outdoor order — [monsoon notes](/en/blog/langkawi-monsoon-season-car-rental).
