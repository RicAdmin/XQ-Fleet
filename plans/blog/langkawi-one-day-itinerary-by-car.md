## Editorial Thesis

One strong day is SkyCab early, one north or east nature stop, and a Cenang evening — not every attraction listed online.

## Answer-First Promise

Start at Oriental Village for first cable-car slots, pick either Kilim or a waterfall/north beach, and be back in Cenang before dinner traffic — leave the rest for day two.

## Reader

Transit visitors and short stays with exactly one full driving day.
