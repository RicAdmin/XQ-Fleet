## Editorial Thesis

Scooters win for solo Cenang hops; a car wins when weather, luggage, night returns, or north-island range enter the plan.

## Answer-First Promise

Rent a scooter only if you are a licensed, confident rider staying local to Cenang; otherwise the car’s rain protection and Grab-alternative coverage pay for themselves.
