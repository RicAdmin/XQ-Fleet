## Editorial Thesis

The Kok–Telaga Harbour–Datai stretch is the quiet scenic half-day most Cenang-based itineraries skip; fuel first and watch for wildlife.

## Answer-First Promise

Leave mid-morning with a full tank, loop Telaga Harbour and Datai, and optionally continue toward Tanjung Rhu — expect rainforest roads, not a beach-bar strip.

## Reader
