## Editorial Thesis

An IDP is a translation companion to your home licence under the 1949 Geneva framework Malaysia recognises for visitors; you need it when the counter or police cannot clearly read your licence, and you should carry both documents together.

## Answer-First Promise

If your physical home licence is clearly readable in English Latin script, many short-stay rentals proceed on the licence alone — but an IDP removes debate. If the licence is regional-script only or hard to verify, get a 1949 IDP before you fly; softcopy alone is not enough at pickup.

## Reader

First-time foreign licence holders preparing documents for Langkawi, including anyone whose home licence is bilingual, regional-script, or unfamiliar to Malaysian counters.
