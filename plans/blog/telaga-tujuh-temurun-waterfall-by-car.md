## Editorial Thesis

Any standard automatic rental reaches Telaga Tujuh and Temurun; the useful plan is parking, climb effort, fuel before the northwest, and combining with SkyCab without backtracking.

## Answer-First Promise

Drive Temurun on the Datai road first if you want a short walk, then Telaga Tujuh near Oriental Village if you have legs for stairs — fill fuel before you leave Cenang or Kuah.
