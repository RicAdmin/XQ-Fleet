## Editorial Thesis

For a Kuah shopping day, boot litres matter more than seat count until the third row is occupied; Bezza-class sedans (~508 L) handle most duty-free hauls, while Alza-class MPVs win when people and bags compete for the same space.

## Answer-First Promise

Couples and small families with shopping bags: choose a sedan with a large fixed boot (Bezza / Vios / City class). Four-plus adults plus luggage plus shopping: fold the Alza third row or start in an MPV — don’t expect an Axia hatch to swallow a chocolate-and-bottle run.
